Header control for refs/open-plan/rolling-updates. Refresh from main is automatic; the control's job is to show sync state and let you publish pending ambient edits. Pick a state, flip the theme, try the layouts.

What each color means

In sync — nothing to publish. muted
Pending — ambient edits ready for main. info / blue
Syncing — publish or auto-rebase running. spinner
Blocked — a refresh conflict paused sync. warn / amber
Offline — daemon down, control disabled. dim

Colors reuse your existing convention (emerald=good, amber=warning from connection-status); blue is the only addition, for “action available.”

My recommendation

B for the header, C behavior on click

The bare icon (A) is elegant but hides what you’re about to merge into main — and publish is consequential and hard to eyeball-verify. Keep A’s minimalism as the resting state, add B’s count pill for at-a-glance pending volume, and make the click open C’s review popover rather than publishing blind. That preserves your “one control in the header” instinct while giving publish a one-beat confirm and giving conflicts somewhere to live.
